Number Please

Your household's telephone exchange.

Number Please turns a vintage telephone into a household assistant. Lift the handset and you hear a dial tone; dial 0 and an operator answers. Ask her the time, the weather or the news, add things to the shopping list, set a reminder, or switch the lights off, all by talking on an old-fashioned phone.

It is a private, hobby project run by one household for its own use. It isn't a commercial service and there is no sign-up: only the household's own Google accounts can sign in.

Google sign-in and Google Home

Household members sign in with Google, so the exchange never stores a password. A household owner may also connect the household's Google Home, so the operator can read and control the household's own lights and thermostats when a caller asks. The privacy policy explains exactly what is used and how.
